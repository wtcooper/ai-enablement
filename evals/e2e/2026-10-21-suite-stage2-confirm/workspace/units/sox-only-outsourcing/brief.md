# Unit brief: SOX-only outsourcing (15 clients)
Unit id: sox-only-outsourcing · Parent: ../../transformation-plan.md · Owner: practice lead · Wave: 1 · Planning mode: same user (proposed)

## Why this unit
The 15 SOX-only outsourcing clients are the proposed first place to act: independence is simpler and the firm controls the workpaper format (user's view, to test). About 210 control tests a month, about 60 of them ITGC (tracking sheet, as relayed). About 30% draw a review note and about two-thirds of those are evidence completeness or IPE issues. Nobody owns report validation, IT audit specialists are billed to integrated engagements, and there is no shared place to keep a validated report.

## What we already know
- Sample case (user access recertification, ERP): about 11 days from request to first review finished, about 13 with rework; about 2.5 h staff hands-on; three review notes. Reported, one case.
- Tracking sheet aggregates: about 210 tests last month, about 60 ITGC, about 30% drawing a note, about two-thirds of those on evidence completeness or IPE (about 20% of all tests). From record, as relayed; not seen by the advisor.
- Unwritten senior checks: listing straight from the system (not edited in Excel); date range covers the full period; the active-user population used for comparison is complete. Stated.
- Report-validation sizing: about 25 key controls per client, about 8 distinct key reports, about 3 h per report, about 360 specialist hours across 15 clients (user estimates; sensitivity about 180-600 h).
- Contrast: one integrated-audit case, with report parameters tested once by an IT audit specialist, took about 1.5 h, drew no notes and waited about 2 days for review. Reported, one case.

## Inherited settings (change only with the parent owner's agreement)
Archetype: assurance · Module: controls-testing · Levers and weights: capacity 0.25, quality 0.25, risk 0.20, staff experience 0.20, margin 0.10 · Scoring weights: value 0.30, feasibility 0.20, data readiness 0.15, risk 0.20, time to value 0.05, strategic fit 0.10 · Capacity stance: redeploy (no reduction in staff headcount; not a commitment on fees) · Guardrails: a person owns evidence selection, sufficiency conclusions, deficiency evaluation and sign-off; a senior or manager reviews every AI-flagged exception and a sample of unflagged items; independence rules unchanged; client data only in approved tools; every AI-assisted step traceable and reperformable.

## Shared definitions and common measures
- Control test: one control tested against its attributes for one period, as counted in the tracking sheet.
- Review-note rate: share of control tests drawing at least one senior review note.
- Rework: any test sent back to staff after review, for any reason.
- Hands-on time per control test: staff hours from evidence in hand to workpaper ready for review (senior review hours tracked separately).
- Elapsed time: request-list item out to review finished, and again to final sign-off after rework.
- Common measures every unit reports: hands-on hours per control test, review-note rate, days from request to final sign-off.

## Questions this plan must answer
1. Which steps should stop being done per test (for example re-proving report completeness), and which should be done once and reused?
2. What would a written pre-review check for evidence completeness look like, and who would own report validation?
3. Which lookup and drafting steps could a certified tool take, with the human checks in the guardrails?
4. How do juniors keep learning the seniors' unwritten checks if a tool takes the first pass?
5. What would the clients' external auditors need to see before relying on this work?

## What to prepare
Last month's counts by control type and the worst week; the number of review notes by type for one month (counts only); two or three real examples of the work, with no client-confidential data; the current workpaper template and test-step wording; the roles that do the work; what the audit platform and the enterprise chat assistant can do today, and any unapproved tool use (asked without judgment).

## How to run it
Proposed: the practice lead goes deep on this unit directly in this working folder, with the ITGC senior manager (register owner and validator) joining for validation of the map. The pilot working lead role was dropped. Results go to the parent plan.

## What the parent needs back
Opportunities using the shared vocabulary, value as ranges in the common measures with assumptions, risks, human checkpoints and role evolution (including junior development) for each opportunity.
