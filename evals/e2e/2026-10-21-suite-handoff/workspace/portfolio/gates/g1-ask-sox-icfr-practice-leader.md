**To:** SOX/ICFR practice leader
**From:** Head of AI
**About:** Protected senior time for the pre-review checklist

---

You are the benefit owner and, we are proposing, the sponsor for the pre-review evidence checklist for SOX-only testing. It passed the screen on the basis that it cuts evidence-completeness review notes and the second-round reviews they cause. It is a written checklist in our existing SOX workpaper templates. It involves no AI and no client data. Everything else on it depends on senior time, so I need two things from you.

**1. Two or three protected senior half-days, in the shoulder weeks and none in peak.** In them two seniors write down the checks they run in their heads at review: that the listing came straight from the system, that it covers the full period, that the comparison population is complete, the sign-off date against policy, and the trace from each exception to its ticket. The methodology owner then approves the checklist.

**2. Two named seniors to do it.** I'd like you to choose them, since you know who has the time and the standing to write the checks down.

What I'm asking you to release is time. There is no spend, and the checklist stays with the methodology owner after launch.

What comes next, once the checklist is written: the ITGC senior manager back-tests it blind on past files, without seeing the PCAOB comment text. If it would have caught under 25% of the evidence-completeness notes, we stop. If the half-days can't be released, we also stop at the gate rather than let it drift.

**One timing point.** The ITGC senior manager is also the proposed validator for the first stage of the report register, at about four hours a week outside peak. I don't want to pull in a second role or double up that one, so the back-test will wait until the register's first-stage workload leaves room. Writing the checklist does not wait for it.

**One thing I need your view on:** is the sponsor role right for you? The forum has not yet agreed it, and I'm agreeing it with them now. I don't want to wait on that before the half-days are fixed.

I'd also like the tracking-sheet records of review notes by cause for the SOX-only line. That sets the baseline we judge the checklist against.

Can you tell me whether the half-days can be released, and who the two seniors are?
