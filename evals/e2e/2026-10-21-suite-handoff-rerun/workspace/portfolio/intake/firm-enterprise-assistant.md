## The problem in one sentence

Staff across practices already use the one approved assistant on non-client material, but nobody records its owner, what it is used for or how to switch it off; logging it as a live system gives the firm an owner, a use list and a safe switch-off.

## How often it happens

Not known. IT holds a licence count for the assistant (reported; the count was not given in this session). Which practices use it, for what, and how often is not recorded. The audit plan says a few named seniors use it weekly-ish on non-client work (reported, not verified).

## Class

individual-productivity. A seat or licence roll-out belongs on the enablement line (ai-enablement), so this entry is an inventory record for visibility. It is not scored as a portfolio win and its licence count is not a benefit.

## Rules or AI

AI by definition (it is the assistant). The portfolio question here is ownership, use and exit, not whether AI is needed.
