## Error patterns seen

- **Attribution drift** was the most common pattern, found 8 times: IRS headcount, the Multiplier and Crete investor,
  Black Ore and BLS, the summit forecast, WCG pricing, CPA Trendlines and TaxDome, the MIT thesis, and the TR framing.
- **Embellished qualifiers and causes**, found 9 times: "directly", "tested", "presented", "debated", "moderating",
  "automated", "large exposed", "as automation…", and "most by".
- **Misread statistics**, found 4 times: the Ignition average, McKinsey as an annual global figure, MIT per standard
  deviation, and KPMG compliance versus return preparation.
- **Wrong or missing dates**, found 4 times: Blue J, TR fielding dates, EY Tax News, and Karbon.
- **Out-of-date status**: the EU AI Act was still described as provisional after adoption.
- **Forecasts presented as facts or misfiled**: the IRS CEO's five-year aim mixed with this year's headcount, and TR's
  framework filed under "Forecast".
- **Unsourced connective claims**: the planning-conversation framing, "low base", and the authority-follow-up inference.
- **Regulatory precision**: §10.27(a) versus §10.27(b), PTIN representation rights, and the SSN offshore rule.
