### Professional bodies

- **Center for Audit Quality (CAQ):** Audit Partner Pulse, Audit Insider newsletter, comment letters [CAQ-PULSE-2026;
  CAQ-INSIDER-2026; CAQ-404-2026].
- **AICPA and CPA.com:** Audit Transformation Report (a 54-firm US benchmark, firms of varying sizes), Journal of Accountancy
  [CPACOM-ATR-2025; JOFA-AIPA-2026].
- **ICAEW:** viewpoints, tests of GenAI on audit tasks, podcasts [ICAEW-PIERPOINT-2025; ICAEW-FRCVIEW-2026].
- **The IIA and Internal Audit Foundation:** Pulse of Internal Audit, comment letters [JOFA-IAPULSE-2026; IIA-PULSE-2024].
- **ISACA:** AI Pulse Poll; AAIA certification for AI auditors [ISACA-PULSE-2026; ISACA-NA-2026].
- **Accountancy Europe:** private equity in audit; GenAI comments [ACCTEU-PE-2026].
- **ACCA, CPA Australia, CPA Canada, ICAS, IFAC Knowledge Gateway:** playbooks and commentary (not re-checked).
- **Cloud Security Alliance:** SOC 2 and AI assurance research notes (low authority; leads only) [CSA-AIASSURE-2026].

### Journals and research centres

- **Journals:** *Auditing: A Journal of Practice & Theory*, *Contemporary Accounting Research*, *The Accounting Review*,
  *Journal of Accounting Research*, *Review of Accounting Studies*, *Accounting Horizons*, *Journal of Emerging
  Technologies in Accounting*, *Journal of Information Systems*, *International Journal of Accounting Information
  Systems*, *Current Issues in Auditing*. Not re-verified at this date.
- **Centres:** Rutgers Continuous Auditing and Reporting Lab, University of Duisburg-Essen, BYU, Hamburg, Tilburg, PCAOB
  Office of Economic and Risk Analysis, Stanford Digital Economy Lab (labour effects) [STANFORD-CANARIES-2026].
- **Preprints and benchmarks:** arXiv audit benchmarks (FinancialAuditBench and others); SSRN field experiments. Check
  authors' affiliations (for example, FinancialAuditBench is vendor-authored) [ARXIV-FAB-2026].

### Publications, podcasts and communities

- **Trade press:** Accounting Today, Journal of Accountancy, CPA Practice Advisor, CFO Dive, CFO Brew, Bloomberg Law and
  Bloomberg Tax, The Accountant, Corporate Board Member, Thomson Reuters Tax & Accounting news, Computerworld (for FT
  analyses). Financial Times, Wall Street Journal and Going Concern are often paywalled; many figures arrive through
  retellings, so label them.
- **Podcasts:** ICAEW *Beyond Finance* ("How should audit firms use AI?", Nov 2025); CPA Practice Advisor *Accounting
  Technology Lab*; IESBA *Decoding Ethics*; *The Audit Podcast*; *The Accounting Podcast* [ICAEW-POD-2025].
- **Communities:** IIA AuditSphere, ISACA chapters, CAQ's "Audit in Action" blog, r/Accounting and r/auditing (often
  blocked to automated access), LinkedIn. Little usable community evidence was found at this date.

### Vendors and analysts to watch

Treat every figure from these as vendor-published.
- **Audit platforms and agents:** Caseware, Thomson Reuters, DataSnipper, MindBridge, Fieldguide, Inflo, Validis,
  Suralink [CASEWARE-VERITY-2026; TR-DISCLOSURE-2026; DATASNIPPER-2026; MINDBRIDGE-ARA-2026; FIELDGUIDE-2026].
- **Confirmations and bank data:** Circit, Thomson Reuters Confirmation [CIRCIT-2026].
- **Controls and internal audit:** Optro (formerly AuditBoard), Workiva, Andera, Petual [OPTRO-CRX-2026; ANDERA-RSM-2026].
- **SOC platforms:** Vanta, Drata (see [soc-attestation](modules/soc-attestation.md)).
- **Analysts:** Gartner (CAE surveys), Verdantix (pricing, ESG assurance) [GARTNER-2026; VERDANTIX].

### Where the evidence is thin

- Independent accuracy, error or override rates for any audit AI tool in live use: none found.
- Measured effects of AI on audit hours, fees, staffing mix or margins at firm level: none found.
- AI in acceptance decisions, specialists' work, going concern, EQR, KAMs and opinion drafting: little or none.
- Sustainability assurance providers' own AI use: none found.
- Smaller firms outside one 54-firm US benchmark; non-Big Four platform details.
- Primary texts often behind logins: the AICPA practice aid, SOC guidance, PCAOB pages.
