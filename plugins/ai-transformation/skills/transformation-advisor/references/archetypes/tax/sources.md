### Where the evidence is thin

- Independent error, override or parallel-run rates for any commercial tax AI tool in live use: none found.
- Measured effects of AI on fees, hours, staffing mix or margins in tax: none found.
- AI in the provision (ASC 740 and IAS 12) with a named user and a result; auditing AI-prepared tax balances: none found.
- In-house case studies with named metrics; practitioner-side Appeals and litigation AI.
- Section 7216 and AI; reasonable cause and AI reliance; tax-practitioner sanctions for AI misuse: no authority found.
- Markets outside the US, UK, Australia and Germany; trusts and estates, payroll, customs and state and local tax.
- Primary texts often behind paywalls or logins: Tax Notes, Bloomberg Tax, the full Thomson Reuters, Fonoa, Blue J and
  EY reports, TEI member sessions.
