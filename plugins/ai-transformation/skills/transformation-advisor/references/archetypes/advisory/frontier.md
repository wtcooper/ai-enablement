## 1. How to read the signals

- **Fact, plan or forecast.** An adopted standard or a closed deal is a fact; a launch or alliance is a plan until
  customers report results; "by 2030" is a forecast by an interested party. The tables say which.
- **Most of what is moving is rules, products and ownership**, not proven economics: no source measures an AI-driven
  change in advisory fees, margins or error rates ([state-of-ai.md](state-of-ai.md) §7).
- **Ask of each signal:** does it change what we must do (rules), what we can do (tools), who we compete with
  (entrants and capital), what clients buy (demand), or who will do the work (talent)?
