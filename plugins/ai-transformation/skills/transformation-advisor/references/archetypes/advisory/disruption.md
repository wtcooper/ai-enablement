# Advisory disruption scenarios

Last researched 2026-10-21. Use this at unit or firm scope, in Frame (how exposed is this business) and in Reimagine
lens 4 (reinvent). Each scenario separates what exists, what is forecast and what would have to be true. No source
assigns probabilities; neither does this file. The scenarios are not exclusive: a firm can see compliance fees
compress, CAS grow, an AI-native entrant take small-client bookkeeping and platform consolidation in one planning
period. Firm-wide scenarios (outside capital, the pyramid) are in
[professional-services disruption](../professional-services/disruption.md). Source keys in brackets resolve in
[sources.md](sources.md).

## 2. Scenario 1: the fee model shifts

**The picture.** AI compresses routine work, so time-based fees fall away and work is sold as fixed, subscription,
value or outcome-linked fees.

- **Exists.** Fixed fee (66%) and hourly (60%) still dominate, and 68% of 200+ firm leaders were ruling out new pricing
  or undecided; about one in five of those already on subscription or value pricing say AI drove it
  [BILL-AIAMBITION-2026]. RKL is piloting value pricing with "several dozen" clients [BBLAW-PRICING-2026]. Digits
  prices firms on achieved automation [DIGITS-OUTCOME-2026].
- **Forecast.** 69% of tax professionals anticipate value-based, hybrid or fixed fees (Blue J survey)
  [CPACOM-BLUEJ-2026]; a firm CEO forecasts outcome-linked advisory fees [BBTAX-SH-2026]. GGI argues judgment-heavy work
  keeps time-based billing while routine work moves to fixed or outcome fees (opinion) [MONDAQ-GGI-2026].
- **Friction.** Contingent fees are barred or restricted for audit clients ([guardrails.md](guardrails.md) §4); in tax
  matters the IRS OPR says savings should be passed on openly [IRS-OPR-2026].
- **What would have to be true.** Baselines per metric, system data, an eligible-client rule, and a decision on who keeps
  the saving.
- **For the leader.** Decide deliberately where the gain lands; under fixed fees it lands with the firm until the
  client renegotiates. Detail: [economics.md](economics.md) §6.

## 3. Scenario 2: AI-native providers and platforms

**The picture.** New providers deliver bookkeeping, QoE or SOX testing with agents and a few people, selling direct or
supplying capacity to firms.

- **Exists.** Pilot sells an AI Accountant it calls fully autonomous direct to small businesses and runs a local
  partner programme (CAS `#16`, `#17`). Shepi lists a $1,000 self-service QoE [SHEPI-PRICING-2026]; QoEAgent and Shepi
  offer white-label capacity to firms (transactions `#05`, `#06`). Petual and Midship sell agent-run SOX testing
  (risk-controls `#15`). Basis raised $100M to supply firm-side agents [BASIS-SERIESB-2026]; Atlas positions AI as a
  "junior accountant" in partner firms, claiming 5X gains on targeted workflows (self-reported) [ATLAS-SEED-2026].
- **Fragility.** Botkeeper announced its closure in February 2026, citing consolidation among its largest clients, and
  Xendoo bought the platform [CPAPA-XENDOO-2026].
- **Incumbents partner.** CLA is co-building a firm-trained model with Digits for its CAAS practice [ACCTODAY-CLA-2026].
- **Quality pressure.** Forvis Mazars US says AI is increasing lower-quality, lower-cost QoE products that can look
  professional [FORVIS-QOE-2026]. An AI consultant says autonomy only works for constrained, simple cases today (CAS
  module §9).
- **What would have to be true.** Independent accuracy data; lenders, auditors and courts accepting the output (per
  QoEAgent's reading of the SBA rule, software cannot be the QoE provider [QOEAGENT-WHO-2026]).
- **For the leader.** Choose to build, buy or partner per line, and keep exports and an exit plan.

## 4. Scenario 3: capital and consolidation

**The picture.** PE-backed platforms and AI-native roll-ups buy firms and fund AI centrally; scale becomes the way to
pay for technology.

- **Exists.** IFAC counts 1,052 PE-linked transactions in 2015-2025 [CPAPA-IFACPE-2026]; CPA Trendlines tracks about
  $49B of enterprise value to May 2026 [CPATRENDLINES-TRACKER-2026]. Grant Thornton Advisors' $5B CBIZ deal is tied to
  funding AI [GT-CBIZ-2026]. Current reports $428M revenue as an AI-backed roll-up [ACCTODAY-CURRENT-2026]; Multiplier
  says AI doubled an acquired firm's margins (company claim) [TECHCRUNCH-MULTIPLIER-2025].
- **What buyers buy.** Forensic, valuation, cyber and IT capabilities in April 2026, read by the tracker as
  "defensible advisory niches" [CPATRENDLINES-TRACKER-2026].
- **Counter-views.** Current's CEO contrasts "cost extraction on a deadline" with long-hold growth
  [ACCTODAY-CURRENT-2026]; IFAC lists higher fees and more investment in advisory than audit as possible risks, not
  findings [CFOBREW-PE-2026].
- **Independence consequence.** Outside capital brings the attest and advisory split, which limits what can be sold to
  attest clients; the AICPA rule is still moving [CROWE-KKR-2026; JOFA-PEEC-2026].
- **For the leader.** An approach from a buyer is also an AI decision: whose platform, whose data, and which services
  the attest arm can no longer buy from the advisory arm.

## 5. Scenario 4: clients do more themselves

**The picture.** Client finance, audit and deal teams run their own agents and buy less outside help.

- **Exists.** Reddit and Klaviyo hand defined SOX tests to agents in house, and Havertys starts moving outsourced IT
  SOX testing to agents (risk-controls `#05`, `#08`, `#14`). BlackLine, FloQast, Microsoft and Workday have shipped,
  previewed or announced close agents [CPAPA-AGENTSCLOSE-2026]. 31% of PE, VC and LP respondents report AI integrated in
  diligence [SPGLOBAL-PE-2026].
- **Budget signal.** Among AI-using middle-market leaders raising AI spend, 40% take budget from external consulting;
  the survey does not say what replaces it [CPAPA-RSMMM-2026].
- **Practitioner view.** A consultant says clients already arrive with AI-prepared work [ACCTODAY-CATALYST-2026]; a
  fractional CFO says some clients probably will use AI for the accounting, though he has seen little desire so far
  [CFODIVE-FRACTIONAL-2026]. No quantified revenue loss from client self-service was found.
- **For the leader.** Move toward the work clients still pay for: review, interpretation, judgment, and controls over
  the client's own agents (Scenario 6).

## 6. Scenario 5: recurring work becomes continuous advisory

**The picture.** Automated bookkeeping frees CAS capacity, and the clean, current ledger becomes the platform for
dashboards, forecasting and advice sold on subscription.

- **Facts.** The Journal of Accountancy describes CAS as moving the firm from transactional provider to an embedded,
  ongoing resource [JOFA-CASTIPS-2025]. Withum says advisory "sells best next to CAS" and uses AI for variance finding and real-time
  dashboards [ACCTODAY-CASKEYS-2026]. Manual data cleanup is the top advisory blocker (30%) [INTUIT-ACCTTECH-2026]. The
  Choi and Xie field study associates genAI with effort moving from data entry to communication and quality assurance
  [STANFORD-JAR-2026].
- **Forecast.** Tom Hood (AICPA & CIMA) forecast a move from personal-productivity AI to managing finance agents
  [JOFA-HOOD-2026]; Withum's Jim Bourke predicts near-complete automation of the tax process [ACCTODAY-CASKEYS-2026].
- **Not found.** Any independent measure of CAS-to-advisory conversion. Advisory slowed at the largest firms in 2025
  Top 100 data [WOODARD-TWR-2025].
- **For the leader.** Test it on your own book: which clean clients bought advisory last year, and at what fee basis?

## 7. Scenario 6: new services around AI

**The picture.** Clients' own AI creates new work: governing it, testing it, investigating its misuse and preparing for
assurance over it.

- **Exists.** AI governance, control design and certification-readiness advisory (risk-controls `#17`); internal-audit
  review of a bank's AI governance (risk-controls `#19`); advising CAS clients on their AI adoption (CAS `#20`); family
  AI policies (private-client `#14`). Grant Thornton launched Enterprise Assurance Services with provisional AIUC-1
  accreditation, and the AICPA and AIUC announced a SOC 2 plus AIUC-1 engagement; the assurance itself is attest work
  [GT-EAS-2026; JOFA-AIUC-2026].
- **Demand claimed.** Grant Thornton's CEO says governance demand is already here (no figures) [GT-CBIZ-2026]; 42% of
  large finance functions are fully assurance-ready [KPMG-AIFINANCE-2026]; only 7% of anti-fraud professionals feel
  more than moderately prepared for AI-fuelled fraud [ACFE-TECHBENCH-2026].
- **Ideas, not yet sold.** Deepfake examination, AI-fuelled fraud and AI-washing investigations, and validation studies
  for evidence rules (forensic-disputes `#11`, `#12`, `#15`); forecast back-testing as a service (fpa-performance `#14`).
- **What would have to be true.** Paid engagements with defined scope; independence checked where the client is also
  an attest client; staff who are both auditor and technologist.

## 8. Exposure by service line

| Service line | Threat | Change a lot | Mainly help | Why (see module) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CAS bookkeeping and close | High for simple, small-client books | Clients per accountant, pricing, offshore model | Complex multi-entity and controllership work | AI-native bookkeeping sells direct; close agents ship |
| Fractional CFO and controllership | Low | Reporting and forecasting preparation | Advice and AI guidance to clients | Demand for interim finance leaders rising |
| Risk and controls | Medium for outsourced testing hours | Testing model, review, sourcing | AI governance and readiness work | In-house agents and AI-native testing vendors |
| Transactions | Medium for entry-level QoE | Data work, databook, pricing | Judgment on adjustments, sell-side readiness | Cheap AI QoE; buyers' first pass; lender rules |
| Valuation | Low to medium | Research, drafting, review, fund marks | Defence of the work | Standards require disclosure; platforms sell recurring marks |
| Forensic and disputes | Low | Data preparation, review | New AI-fraud and deepfake matters | Evidence law sets the pace |
| FP&A and performance | Medium for routine reporting | Implementation work, managed FP&A | Readiness and data work | Clients' own planning agents |
| Private client | Low | Alternatives intake, reporting | Policy, controls and relationships | Data sensitivity keeps work human-led |

These ratings are the advisor's reading of the evidence above, not a source's forecast. Revisit them with the leader's
own revenue mix.
