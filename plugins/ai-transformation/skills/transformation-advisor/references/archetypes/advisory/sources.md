### Firms, platforms and vendors to watch

- **Firms:** RSM, Grant Thornton Advisors and CBIZ, Crowe, BDO, CLA, Withum, CohnReznick, Cherry Bekaert, Aprio, Citrin
  Cooperman, Forvis Mazars, PKF O'Connor Davies, Armanino, Stout, Kroll, J.S. Held, and the Big Four deal and risk groups.
- **Platforms and vendors:** Digits, Pilot, Basis, Ramp, BlackLine, FloQast, Intuit, Xero, Sage (CAS); Fieldguide,
  Optro, Andera, Petual, Midship (risk); Opio, ToltIQ, QoEAgent, Shepi (transactions); Valutico, BVR, 73 Strings
  (valuation); Relativity, Valid8 (forensic); Abacum, OneStream, Rillet (FP&A); Asseta, Masttro (private client).
- **AI-native roll-ups and entrants:** Current (formerly Crete), Multiplier, Atlas; Botkeeper as the cautionary case.

### Where the evidence is thin

- No firm publishes error, override, parallel-run or fee-model data for AI-assisted advisory work.
- Transaction advisory, forensic, FP&A and private client rest on vendor marketing, firm announcements and a few
  practitioner statements; no adoption survey covers them at accounting firms.
- The AICPA Code text on nonattest services (ET 1.295) and contingent fees was login-gated; independence treatment for
  private-company attest clients rests on secondary descriptions.
- Conference catalogues for ENGAGE, Digital CPA, the AICPA FVS conference and the ACFE Global conference were not
  retrievable; session detail is partial.
- Due after this research: the 2026 CAS Benchmark Survey report (December 2026), the final IVS text (January 2027), the
  second APS exposure draft and the IESBA PE outcome.
