### Vendors and firms to watch

- **Legal platforms:** Harvey, Legora, Thomson Reuters CoCounsel, LexisNexis, Microsoft Copilot for Legal; lab
  offerings from Anthropic, Google and OpenAI [LAWSITES-MSFT-2026; HARVEY-ORCH-2026; LEGORA-LAUREL-2026].

  iManage and NetDocuments (documents), Introhive (relationships).
- **PSA and project firms:** Certinia, Kantata, Planview, Deltek, Rocketlane [CERTINIA-VEDA-2026; KANTATA-RMI-2026].
- **Firms whose public statements move the debate:** PwC, McKinsey, Accenture, KPMG, Deloitte; Hogan Lovells
  Cadwalader, Kirkland & Ellis, Debevoise; Crowe; AI-native entrants (Crosby, Garfield.Law, Falcon, General Legal,
  Ascend) [CPAPA-PWC-2026; BI-MCKINSEY-2025; LEGORA-HLC-2026; AT-CROWE-2026].
- Treat vendor customer stories as claims: most named-firm figures in the modules sit on vendor pages.

### Where the evidence is thin

- **Consulting primary sources:** McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Deloitte, EY and KPMG pages were blocked or empty; their
  figures rest on press and secondary summaries.
- **Measured outcomes:** no firm publishes error, override, realisation or margin effects of AI; most "hours saved"
  figures are self-reported or vendor-published.
- **Engineering and architecture:** one annual benchmark (Deltek) and two ethics or guidance texts.
- **In-house beyond legal:** finance, HR and other shared-service teams working like firms are almost undocumented.
- **Mid-tier and small firms:** data skew to large law firms and vendor customers.
- **Primary texts not reached:** SRA pages (the August 2026 warning notice was seen only through a DWF article), AICPA
  and IESBA AI guidance, the full NBER paper (abstract and disclosures only), SPI and Source full reports. ABA Formal
  Opinion 512 was read in full from a copy hosted by LawNext, not from the ABA site.
