# Professional-services interview guide

Last researched 2026-10-19. Use this alongside the method files: onboarding questions come from
[onboarding.md](../../method/onboarding.md), question craft from [interviewing.md](../../method/interviewing.md). This
file gives the professional-services wording for each firm type, the module chooser, boundary probes, phase questions
and the vocabulary to mirror. Ask one question at a time and skip what you already know. Each module file has its own
interview probes in §4; use those once a module is in focus. Source keys in brackets resolve in §10 or in
[sources.md](sources.md).

## 1. Who you are likely talking to

| Person | What they usually care about | Open with |
|---|---|---|
| Managing partner, CEO or firm chair | Clients, pricing, partner economics, competitors, capital | "Why look at AI now, and what would the firm look like in three years if it went well?" |
| Practice or service-line leader | Margin, staffing, quality, client demands, junior development | "Which part of a typical matter or engagement eats the most senior time?" |
| COO, CFO or finance director | Realisation, write-downs, utilisation, AI spend and its return | "When AI saves hours on a matter, where does that show up in your numbers today?" |
| Pricing or client-value lead | Fee mix, AFAs, client AI-savings requests, rate rounds | "What share of fees is hourly, and what are clients asking you to change?" |
| Innovation, knowledge or AI lead | Tools, adoption, data, governance, vendor costs | "Which tools are licensed, who uses them weekly, and how do you know they work?" |
| Risk, general counsel or quality partner | Confidentiality, client terms, supervision, insurance, incidents | "What does your AI policy say, and how would you show a court or a client it was followed?" |
| Talent or learning lead | Intake, training, the apprenticeship, evaluation | "What did first-years do three years ago that AI now does, and what teaches judgment instead?" |
| Bid, BD or marketing lead | Proposal volume, win rates, client relationships | "How many proposals a year, and how do you decide which ones to bid?" |
| General counsel or legal-operations head (in house) | Outside counsel spend, intake, insourcing, budget | "Which work do you send out today that you would like to keep in house?" |

## 2. Onboarding questions in the field's words

Use these in place of, or after, the starred questions in onboarding §3.

| Profile element | Question | Listen for |
|---|---|---|
| Role and scope | "Are you responsible for your own clients, a team, a practice, an office, the firm, or an in-house function?" | Scope level; firm or in house |
| What is delivered | "What do clients get from you: advice, documents, analysis, a design, a decision, a filing?" | Which modules; whether assurance, tax or security also apply |
| Clients | "Who are the clients, and how concentrated is the work in the top ten?" | Buyer power; client AI terms |
| How it is paid | "Is work priced by the hour, fixed fee, capped, retainer, subscription or on outcomes? Roughly what share each?" | Who keeps an AI saving ([economics.md](economics.md)) |
| Journey stage | "In the last two weeks, who used AI on real client work, for which step? Where is that recorded?" | Real use versus awareness |
| Pressures | "What is pushing hardest: client fee pressure, competitors, talent, partner expectations, capital?" | Why now |
| Constraints | "What must not change: who signs off, confidentiality, client guidelines, your insurer's terms, regulators?" | Guardrails ([guardrails.md](guardrails.md)) |
| Tools and data | "Which systems hold matters, time, documents and client data? PSA, practice management, DMS, CRM?" | Data readiness ([value-chain.md](value-chain.md)) |
| Unapproved use | "In most firms some people use AI tools that haven't been approved. Is that happening here? There's no wrong answer." | Shadow AI: 34% of 1,816 professionals say so [TR-FOP-REPORT-2026] |
| Session goal | "What would make this worthwhile: one quick win, a plan for the practice, or a case for your partners?" | Path |

**Bring evidence in early.** Once you know the focus, give one real example from the relevant module with its grade,
and say who measured it. In most modules the honest answer is "the vendor" or "the firm itself".

## 4. Journey-stage boundary probes

Pick two or three. Markers and gates are in [ARCHETYPE.md](ARCHETYPE.md).

| Boundary | Ask | What decides it |
|---|---|---|
| Curious / Experimenting | "Is AI part of how any step is normally done, on more than one matter or engagement?" | Repeated use on real work |
| | "What does your policy say about client data in AI tools, and what would a junior say it says?" | Lived versus paper policy; formal policy ranges from 57% to 98% of law firms by size [ILTA-TECHSURVEY-2026] |
| Experimenting / Adopting | "For the workflow you trust most, what is the baseline: hours, write-downs, review notes, turnaround?" | An owned, measured workflow |
| | "When AI-assisted work was wrong, who caught it, and how? Where is the check recorded?" | A designed review step, not an intention |
| | "Could you tell a client today what AI changed on their matter?" | Firms often "still gathering the data" [TRI-LEGALWEEK-2026] |
| Adopting / Scaling | "Does your second AI workflow reuse anything from the first: data, review checklist, vendor review?" | Reuse |
| | "Who owns AI here, who pays for usage, and who sees the meter?" | Ownership; AI cost visibility [TRI-METER-2026] |
| Scaling / AI-native | "Has AI changed how you price, staff or scope any service, or let you sell something new?" | Commercial change |
| Delegation check | "Is any agent sending, filing or booking without a person approving each item? What are its written limits?" | The five gates in onboarding §5; oversight scaled to autonomy [CALBAR-GENAI-2026] |

## 5. Phase questions

Use these in addition to each phase file's question bank.

**Frame**
- "If AI halved the hours on your most repeatable work, what would happen to fees, realisation and partner pay?"
- "Who keeps an AI saving today: the firm, the client, or both? Did anyone decide?" (On AI savings, 30% of
  law firms in a vendor pricing survey keep the margin and 30% are still evaluating [BIGHAND-LF-2026].)
- "Which clients have asked about AI, restricted it, or asked for savings?" (Some ask for line items; others will not
  pay for AI-done work [TRI-LEGALWEEK-2026].)
- "Which of your services would AI threaten, change a lot, or mainly help?" ([disruption.md](disruption.md))
- "What does your ownership structure allow if the next three years need a large technology investment?"

**Map**
- "Walk me through one matter or engagement from enquiry to cash. Where do the hours go, by grade?"
- "Where do time, matter, document and client data live, and can you join them?"
- "How many proposals, new matters, engagements and invoices a year? What share is repeat work?"
- "Which steps are offshored, outsourced or done by a central team today?"

**Diagnose**
- "Where does work wait: on partners, on clients, on conflicts, on review?"
- "Which steps need judgment, and which are checks against a document, a playbook or a rule?" (Use the work-type table in
  [value-chain.md](value-chain.md) §3.)
- "Where are write-downs, rejections and rework concentrated?"

**Reimagine**
- "If every first draft arrived agent-prepared with cited sources, what would your reviewers and juniors do?"
- "If a client could buy this as a subscription or a fixed price per document, would you rather sell it or compete with it?"
- "If you started the firm today, what would you charge, whom would you hire, and how many?" (lens 4;
  [disruption.md](disruption.md))

**Prioritize**
- "Which options would your risk partner, insurer or largest client block, and why?"
- "Which need data you do not capture today, such as AI use per matter or outcome measures?"

**Make the case**
- "What is the baseline, measured on real work: hours, write-downs, realisation, turnaround, review notes?"
- "Where would freed hours go: resold, repriced, given back, or into training?" (SPI calls the gap between time
  saved and revenue "the leak" [SPI-AI-2026].)
- "What will the tool cost per seat or per use, and who absorbs a usage overrun?" ([economics.md](economics.md))
- "Where will juniors learn the judgment this step used to teach?" ([workforce.md](workforce.md))

**Plan**
- "Who owns the tool, its vendor review and monitoring, and who owns the workflow's results?"
- "Which clients or matter types go first, and who tells the client?"
- "Which dates fall inside the plan: rate rounds, intake cycles, policy renewals, rule dates?" ([frontier.md](frontier.md))

## 6. Firm-type variants

- **Consultancies:** "What share of revenue is time and materials, fixed fee or outcome-linked?" "Where does your PSA
  hold actuals, and could an agent estimate from them?" "How are partners and directors measured: sales, utilisation,
  margin?" In one vendor-commissioned study only 7.3% of respondents say outcome data is easily accessible and used in staffing [KANTATA-RMI-2026].
- **Accounting and advisory firms (firm side):** "Which advisory services could be priced on value?" "Has private
  equity approached the firm, and what would it fund?" Audit, attestation and tax technical work go to the
  [assurance](../assurance/ARCHETYPE.md) and [tax](../tax/ARCHETYPE.md) archetypes.
- **Engineering and architecture firms:** "What is utilisation by discipline, and where are proposals and project
  reporting done by hand?" Only 38% of respondents in a vendor survey of 896 A&E firms report measurable positive business impact from AI [DELTEK-CLARITY-2026].
- **Security service providers:** pricing, staffing and delivery questions here; detection and response questions in
  the [security](../security/ARCHETYPE.md) archetype.

## 7. In-house and shared-service variants

- "Which outside work could you bring in house with AI, and what stops you?" (68% of 130+ in-house lawyers in a vendor
  survey said more than 10% of outside work could be insourced with AI [JURO-INHOUSE-2026].)
- "Do your outside firms tell you when they use AI, and what it saved?" (59% of 657 in-house respondents saw no GenAI
  savings from firms yet [ACCEVERLAW-2025].)
- "How do requests reach the team, and how do you know what you handled?" ([acceptance-risk](modules/acceptance-risk.md) `#14`)
- "Who funds the tools, and how do you report value to the business?" (GCs naming technology a top strategic priority
  doubled from 2025 to 2026 [TRI-LDOR-2026].)
- Non-legal shared-service teams (finance, HR, procurement) appear almost nowhere in the evidence; say so, and use the
  library's functions for their own work.

## 8. Sensitive topics

- **Client data and matters.** Never ask for client names, documents or figures. Ask for volumes, ranges and roles.
- **Unapproved tools.** Normalise first (§2), after some rapport.
- **Fees and AI savings.** Ask what the policy is, not what a given client was billed.
- **Incidents.** Ask whether the firm has a response plan, not about a specific error.
- **Partner pay and ownership.** Ask about the rules and whether an approach was made, not about terms or individuals.
- **Jobs and intake.** If a leader worries about the team, use [people-and-change.md](../../method/people-and-change.md)
  and [workforce.md](workforce.md); talk about roles and capacity, not headcount, and do not promise outcomes.

## 9. Vocabulary to mirror

| Term | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Matter; engagement; project | The unit of work in law; consulting and accounting; engineering | Use the user's word |
| Realisation; write-down; write-off | Share of worked value collected; value cut before or after billing | Where AI savings leak |
| Utilisation; bench | Share of available time charged to clients; people not assigned | Consulting and A&E |
| Leverage; the pyramid | Juniors per partner; the shape of the firm | Talent and economics |
| AFA; fixed fee; outcome-based fee | Any fee not based on hours; set price; fee tied to results | Pricing |
| OCGs | A client's rules for the firms it hires, now including AI terms | Acceptance and billing |
| Pre-bill (proforma); e-billing; LEDES, UTBMS | Draft bill partners review; client invoice platforms and codes | Billing |
| PSA | Professional services automation: projects, time, resources, billing | Consulting and implementation |
| DMS; KM; precedent | Document management system; knowledge management; reusable past work | Knowledge |
| Ethical wall | A screen that keeps named people, and now AI tools, away from a matter | Acceptance and KM |
| MSO; ABS | Management services organisation; alternative business structure | Outside capital in law |
| Legal operations; CLOC | In-house function running legal as a service; its professional body | In-house |

**Words to avoid:** "AI does the legal work", "replace associates", "robot lawyer", "automated advice". Say instead
"agent-drafted first pass for review", "the responsible professional", "review gate", "human control point".
