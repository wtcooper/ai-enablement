## 1. How to read the signals

- **Fact, plan or forecast.** A judgment, a published code or a closed deal is a fact; a product launch is a plan
  until customers report results; "within two years" is a forecast, usually by an interested party. The tables say
  which.
- **Change with primary evidence is mostly in rules, products and capital**, not in measured economics: no source
  measures an AI-driven change in a firm's realisation or margin ([state-of-ai.md](state-of-ai.md) §7).
- **Ask of each signal:** does it change what we must do (rules), what we can do (tools), who we compete with
  (entrants and capital), what clients will pay for (demand), or who we can hire and train (people)?

  services [TR-FOP-REPORT-2026].

**What a leader should do with this.** Pick the two or three signals that change their own rules, competitors,
clients or people. Put scheduled dates into the plan's milestones, away from rate rounds, year-end and intake cycles.
