# People and change (shared)

This skill uses the suite's shared people-and-change method:
`../../../transformation-advisor/references/method/people-and-change.md`. Read it when you write role evolution
and the redeploy-or-reduce choice (step 3), plan what people are told and trained on (step 5), or when a user
fears for their role.

For a delivered solution, three points from it matter most:

- **Role evolution** names the tasks moving to the solution, the new work, and the capacity choice (redeploy or
  reduce), at the level of roles and capacity, never named people.
- **The junior pipeline:** if the solution takes over work juniors learn from, say what replaces that learning.
- **Communication** in the firm's own terms: what is known, what is not, and what the firm commits to.

The full adoption programme (segments, curriculum, champions, office hours) belongs to the **ai-enablement**
skill. If you can't invoke a skill by name, read `../../../ai-enablement/SKILL.md` and follow it.
