## 1. How to read the signals

- **Fact, plan or forecast.** A published standard or a closed deal is a fact; a firm's roadmap is a plan; "by 2028"
  is a forecast by an interested party. The tables say which.
- **Most of the change with primary-source evidence is regulatory and structural**, not proven economics: no source
  measures an AI-driven fall in audit fees or hours at firm level (see [state-of-ai.md](state-of-ai.md)).
- **Ask of each signal:** does it change what we must do (rules), what we can do (tools), who we compete with
  (entrants), or what clients buy (demand)?
