## 1. How to read the signals

- **Fact, plan or forecast.** A published alert or a closed deal is a fact; a vendor roadmap is a plan; "in five
  years" is a forecast by an interested party. The tables say which.
- **Most change with primary evidence is in rules, products and the authority's own systems**, not in proven
  economics: no source measures an AI-driven fall in tax fees or hours ([state-of-ai.md](state-of-ai.md) §7).
- **Ask of each signal:** does it change what we must do (rules), what we can do (tools), who we compete with
  (entrants), what the authority can see (counterparty), or what clients buy (demand)?
